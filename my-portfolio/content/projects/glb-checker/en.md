## Why I built it

During Proyecto Navarra, reviewing submitted 3D assets repeatedly brought up problems that needed to be resolved before the models could be integrated. **After the project**, I built GLB Checker to make those issues easier to identify and act on.

## An actionable report

The tool analyzes a GLB file and produces a report explaining the problems it detects and what to address. It turns recurring review feedback into guidance that helps people prepare their assets for handoff.

This case study will document the checks the tool performs and show an example of its actionable report.
