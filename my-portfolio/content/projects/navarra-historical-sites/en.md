## Bringing the team's scenes to the web

The project brings three medieval sites in Navarra into an interactive virtual visit: **Zalatambor Castle (Castillo Mayor)** and the **Elgacena Jewish quarter** in Estella-Lizarra, and the **walled settlement of Rada**. Visitors can explore 3D reconstructions alongside images and stories about each place.

I developed **custom scripts to optimize the 3D scenes created by the team for use in the browser**, and created figures and props for the reconstructed interiors.

The gallery shows some of these figures and props within the team's environments, including a woman reading beside a resting dog. For the full reconstruction of Zalatambor Castle, my contribution was optimizing the team's assets for the web.

## Creating figures and props

I started by gathering historical references and selecting details to combine into a coherent visual direction. These references guided the creation of **painting-style images with AI**, which served as the basis for the 3D models.

I then used **Tripo AI** to generate models from the selected images and retouched the results manually to achieve a realistic appearance consistent with the surrounding scene.

The props combine assets generated through the same reference-led process with manual modeling, balancing production speed with the quality and control each object needed. The surrounding environments were created by the team; my contribution brought together asset creation and scripts to prepare their scenes for web delivery.

## An earlier approach: animated characters

Before settling on static figures, I explored AI-generated characters based on existing statues. I cleaned up the models and reworked their textures by hand to avoid the characteristic AI-generated texture look, then prepared and animated them.

The client dropped animation because of time constraints. The statue references also led to a more cartoon-like style than the realistic direction we wanted, so the final static figures used the historical-reference and painting-image workflow described above.
